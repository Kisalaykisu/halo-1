Rocker

Tip over. Don't push off.

The toe sits 34 mm off the ground. The roll starts at 59% of the length, just behind the metatarsal heads, so the shoe tips you forward at the exact moment your calf would start working.

39 mm heel · 32 mm forefoot · 7 mm drop
Upper

One layer. No stretch.

Monofilament woven into 1 mm hexagonal cells. It doesn't give under load and it doesn't hold water, so the fit at 40K is the fit you laced at the start line.

38 g/m² · 1 mm cell · translucent
Halo Rail

A rim, not a plate.

One continuous carbon rim runs the whole perimeter: heel, both sides, toe. The centre stays open, so the foam under your foot compresses freely and springs back in full. A single keystone bridge keeps the rim square under the arch.

Twill carbon · 1.8 mm · 7.2 mm flange
The stack

Five layers. Nothing else.

Soft foam on top, the rail, firm foam underneath, rubber only where you land and toe off. No heel counter, no insole board, no glue you'll ever carry up a hill.

Materials

Four materials. Each one earns its place.

Studio renders of the production model, the same geometry you turned above.

Hex monofilament UPPER · 38 G/M²

Single-layer monofilament woven into 1 mm hexagonal cells. Translucent, non-stretch, unlined. It creases instead of stretching, which is exactly what keeps your foot on the platform in the corners.

AERFOAM X MIDSOLE · TWO LAYERS

Supercritically foamed and split in two so each layer can do one job: the top layer is soft for landing, the bottom layer is firmer so the rail has something to push against.

Halo Rail CARBON TWILL · 7.2 MM FLANGE

A 2×2 twill rim with an upturned flange that shows on the sidewall. Stiff exactly where the foot rolls off and absent where it lands: the centre of the rail is open.

GRIPLINE RUBBER · 1.9 MM

Two pads and nothing more: a chevron-siped forefoot for toe-off and a crescent on the lateral heel for the first touch. Everything else is bare foam you don't have to carry for 42 kilometres.
